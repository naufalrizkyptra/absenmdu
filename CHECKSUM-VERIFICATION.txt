================================================================================
  EXPECTED VISUAL RESULT:
================================================================================

When visiting /login page:

1. INITIAL STATE (0ms):
   - Background: #f8faff (soft indigo white)
   - Logo container appears with subtle bounce
   
2. SHORTLY AFTER (300ms):
   - "Memulai aplikasi..." text fades in from above
   - Text slides down and opacity increases
   
3. LOADING STATE (continuous):
   - Logo rotates continuously (360° per 3 seconds)
   - Multiple concentric rings pulse outward from center
   - Glow effect expands and contracts around logo
   - Three dots appear below message bouncing alternately

Visual layers from back to front:
  Layer 3: Outer rings (faint, slow pulses)
  Layer 2: Middle glow ring (medium intensity)
  Layer 1: Inner glow ring (brightest)
  Layer 0: Logo card with gradient backdrop
